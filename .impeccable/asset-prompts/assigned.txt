A subtle single-dot halftone overlay may sit behind the CTA or as an accent panel, but must not bleed across the photo.

Editorial commerce mood in the lineage of Telfar / SSENSE / i-D: oversized type that behaves like matter, one idea per section at full scale, monumental negative space, monochrome with one unmissable accent. Confident, restrained, lightly playful.
